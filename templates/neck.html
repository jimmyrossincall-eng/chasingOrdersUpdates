Clinical Justification:

  • Cervical pain and stiffness with radicular symptoms
  • Limited range of motion of the cervical spine
  • Muscle spasm and tenderness along the cervical paraspinal musculature

Physical Exam:

  • Tenderness to palpation over cervical spine and paraspinal muscles
  • Reduced cervical flexion, extension, and rotation

Imaging supports cervical disc pathology, foraminal narrowing, and/or degenerative changes consistent with the patient's symptoms.

Medical Necessity:

  • ☑ Immobilize and support the cervical spine to reduce pain
  • ☑ Limit harmful cervical motion during the healing process
  • ☑ Reduce nerve root compression and radicular symptoms
  • ☑ Prevent further cervical injury and promote recovery

Conservative management such as physical therapy, NSAIDs, and muscle relaxants have provided only partial relief.
